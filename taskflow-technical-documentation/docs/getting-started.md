Welcome to TaskFlow! This guide will help you get up and running, even if
you've never used the app before.

## What is TaskFlow?

TaskFlow is a simple, web-based tool for keeping track of your personal
tasks — the things you need to do. You create your own account, and only
you can see and manage your own tasks.

## Creating Your Account

Before you can use TaskFlow, you need to create an account.

1. Go to the registration page.
2. Enter your email address and choose a password.
3. Submit the form to create your account.

A few things to know:

- Your email address is how TaskFlow identifies your account, so each email
  address can only be used to register once. If you try to register with an
  email that's already in use, you'll need to use a different email address
  or log in instead.
- Your password must be at least 8 characters long.
- Creating an account does not log you in automatically. Once your account
  is created, continue to the login step below.

## Logging In

1. Go to the login page.
2. Enter the email address and password you registered with.
3. Submit the form.

If your email and password match an existing account, you'll be taken to
your task list. If either one doesn't match, you won't be let in — double
check for typos and try again.

Once you're logged in, you stay logged in. TaskFlow does not currently
provide a separate log-out option.

## Managing Your Tasks

Once you're logged in, you can create, view, update, complete, and delete
your own tasks. Nobody else can see or change your tasks, and you can't see
anyone else's.

### Creating a Task

1. Choose to add a new task.
2. Enter a **title** — a short summary of the task, such as "Buy
   groceries." A title is required, must be between 1 and 200 characters,
   and can't be blank or made up of just spaces.
3. Optionally, enter a **description** — any extra detail you want to add,
   such as "Milk, eggs, bread." Descriptions can be up to 2,000 characters.
4. Save the task.

Every new task starts out with a status of **pending** — you don't need to
set this yourself.

### Viewing Your Tasks

Your task list shows every task you've created. If you haven't created any
tasks yet, your list will simply be empty until you add one.

### Viewing a Single Task

Open any task from your list to see its full details: title, description,
and current status.

### Updating a Task

You can update a task's title, description, and/or status at any time — you
don't have to change everything at once.

1. Open the task you want to change.
2. Edit the title, description, and/or status.
3. Save your changes.

When you save, TaskFlow keeps track of when the task was last updated. The
same rules from creating a task still apply: the title can't be left blank,
and the title and description still have the length limits described
above.

### Marking a Task as Pending or Completed

Marking a task as completed or pending is simply an update to its status —
there's no separate "complete task" action. Every task has one of two
statuses:

- **Pending** — not done yet. This is the status every task starts with.
- **Completed** — done.

To change a task's status:

1. Open the task you want to change.
2. Set its status to "completed," or back to "pending" if you need to
   reopen it.
3. Save your changes.

### Deleting a Task

1. Open the task you want to remove.
2. Choose the option to delete it.

Once deleted, the task no longer appears in your task list.

## Things to Keep in Mind

- **Email:** must be a valid email address (it must include an `@`), and
  unique to your account — you can't register twice with the same email.
- **Password:** must be at least 8 characters long.
- **Task title:** required, 1–200 characters, can't be blank or just
  spaces.
- **Task description:** optional, up to 2,000 characters.
- **Task status:** can only be "pending" or "completed" — there's no other
  state a task can be in.

If something doesn't work as expected, see the troubleshooting guide.
