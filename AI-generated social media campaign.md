# AI-generated social media campaign

## 1. Objective and scenario

### Objective

Use AI to brainstorm, refine, and plan a complete 7-day social media campaign for a fictional startup. This lab focuses on iterative prompting and prompt chaining to create creative content.

### Scenario

Your team has just been hired by "EcoSip," a fictional startup launching a sleek, reusable, eco-friendly water bottle.

- The mission: The founders are passionate about sustainability but admit they're not great at social media. They've tasked you with creating a 7-day Instagram/TikTok campaign to build hype before launch day. They want a content plan that is fun, relatable, and visually engaging — something that will make people want to talk about the bottle.

- The task: Use an AI tool to brainstorm, refine, and sequence posts.

**Key considerations:**

| Area | Detail |
| --- | --- |
| Target audience | 18–35, eco-conscious, tech-savvy |
| Tone | Fun, relatable, playful, and authentic — avoid generic "save the planet" clichés |
| Platform | Instagram and TikTok |
| Image prompts | Must be usable in AI tools like Midjourney or DALL-E |
| Hashtags | Must be natural, not spammy |

---

## 2. Final deliverables

Submit a single document containing:

1. A 7-day content calendar — for each day include:

   - A post caption (under 100 words)

   - A visual prompt (a description an AI image generator could use)

2. Your prompt history — a log of the prompts you used, showing how you refined them (your "first pass" vs. your "final prompts")

3. A short reflection (200 words or fewer) answering:

   - What changed between your first prompts and your final ones?

   - Why did you make those changes?

   - How did chaining your prompts (using the output of one as the input for another) improve the final result?

---

## 3. Step-by-step instructions

### Phase 1: Understand the task (15 mins)

- Read the scenario and constraints (audience, tone, deliverables)

- Review the evaluation rubric in Section 4 to see how you will be graded

### Phase 2: Deconstruct the problem (30 mins)

Don't try to solve it all in one prompt. Break the task into smaller steps:

- "Brainstorm 7 daily themes for a pre-launch campaign."

- "Write a 50-word, fun caption for [Theme 1]."

- "Create a visual prompt for an AI image tool for [Caption]."

Draft your initial "first pass" prompts for each step.

### Phase 3: Iterative prompting and chaining (60 mins)

- First pass — run your initial prompts and review the output. Is it generic? Is the tone wrong?

- Review — compare the AI's output to the scenario constraints

- Refine — add detail and constraints to your prompts

|  | Example |
| --- | --- |
| Bad prompt | "Write a social media post." |
| Good prompt | "Act as a social media manager for an eco-friendly brand. Your audience is 18–35. Write a 50-word, witty Instagram caption about the frustration of forgetting your reusable bottle. Use 2–3 relevant hashtags." |

- Chain — take the successful output from one prompt and use it as the input for the next

Example: "Here is a caption: [AI-generated caption]. Now, write a visual prompt for an AI image generator that matches this caption's playful and eco-friendly tone."

- Assemble — collect your best outputs into the final 7-day content calendar

### Phase 4: Submit and reflect (15 mins)

- Assemble your final document with the calendar, prompt history, and reflection

- Proofread and submit
